About

☀️ By day, I am a Senior Researcher on the Biomedical ML team at Microsoft Research Health Futures in Cambridge, MA. My work currently centers on epigenomics‑based disease diagnostics and therapeutics, in collaboration with the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard. Previously, I contributed to founding Phenome Health under Dr. Leroy Hood. Before that, I conducted game-theoretic protein design as a Fulbright Research Scholar in Belgium with Dr. Tom Lenaerts. Internships at Microsoft Research, Google, Deloitte, Vanderbilt University, Eli Lilly, and MIT Lincoln Laboratory in biodefense with Dr. Jeremy Kepner.

🌙 By night, I serve on Boards and build programs to empower the next generation of underrepresented groups in science. I am currently a U.S. D-Day Ambassador and an Advisor to flok and Tomorrow's Women Today. Formerly, I served on the MIA Steering Committee at the Broad Institute, as ISCB Student Symposium Finance Chair, as a U.S.-NATO Affairs Delegate, on the Board of Directors of the Fulbright Association, and on the Board of Trustees of AnitaB.org, where I created lasting programs. I am a mother, former college soccer athlete, and Aikido Kokikai practitioner who speaks fluent French and Spanish.
