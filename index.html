About

I am building a world where people age healthily, without protracted illness or disability. With my wet- and dry-lab background, I do this by creating expert-centered interpretable AI methods and interactive platforms that enable more effective human-in-the-loop in-silico hypothesis testing and downstream decision-making in biomedical and clinical settings, integrating genomic, epigenomic, transcriptomic, clinical, and environmental data.

☀️ By day, I am a Senior Researcher on the Biomedical ML team at Microsoft Research Health Futures (New England Research Division) in Cambridge, MA.

🌙 By night, I serve on Boards and develop programs to empower the next generation of underrepresented groups in science.
